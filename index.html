Types of Telugu Action Movies to Explore

There is no single style of Telugu action movie. You can find several subgenres depending on the type of story you enjoy.

1. Crime Action Movies

Most action movies have a crime theme, they are violent, and oftentimes they involve gangs, crime, investigations, or a battle between two powerful individuals.

These movies tend to incorporate suspense and action. The story can be a police officer, gangster, investigator or regular person caught up in a situation that is dangerous.

2. Action Thrillers

Action thrillers are a mix of suspense and high-paced action. The central character might need to find out what has happened, evade a threat or stop an enemy. These are the films to watch if you're looking for both action and an interesting plot.

3. Police Action Movies

Another popular genre of Telugu films are police stories. They are usually determined cops vs criminals and/or strong enemies. The stories can be investigative, chase, confrontational, and dramatic.

4. Revenge Action Movies

In the Indian film industry revenge is a popular theme. These films are usually ones in which a character is going through a huge loss or injustice and is choosing to stand up for themselves. Typically the plot culminates in the final confrontation between the protagonist and the antagonist.

5. Action-Comedy Movies

The genre of action doesn't have to be serious. Action-comedy is a mixture of comedy and action.

For those who prefer not to watch a movie all the way through, but still want some fun, these films may be worth the watch.

What Makes a Good Telugu Action Movie?

The fight scenes are not enough for a good action movie. The story and characters are also significant.

Strong Story

A good plot provides meaning to the action scenes. If the viewers know what the character's objective is, the conflict is more interesting.

Memorable Characters

A movie can be really memorable and stand out if it has a good villain or hero. People will remember characters who have defined motivations.

Well-Placed Action Scenes

It is best to take action if it helps the story rather than just the events of the story. A lot of miscellaneous fight scenes can make a film feel repetitive.

Good Music and Sound

Music and sound design can help to make key scenes more energetic. In particular, the Telugu film industry is known for having a lot of excitement through music and background score.

Good Cinematography

For action scenes, you require good shots in large numbers. Cinematography can be used to depict the size, dynamics and power of a sequence.
